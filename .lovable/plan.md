

# Roma Kalani — Professional Portfolio Website

A clean, professional, text-focused personal website to share with recruiters and head teachers as you apply for Teacher of Mathematics roles.

## Design Direction
- **Light, minimal aesthetic** — white background, clean typography, generous whitespace
- **No heavy images or graphics** — text-driven with subtle use of icons and colour accents
- **Custom Open Graph metadata** — your name, title, and a professional description will appear when sharing the URL (replacing default Lovable branding)
- **Mobile-responsive** — looks great on any device
